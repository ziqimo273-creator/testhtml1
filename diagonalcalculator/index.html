Formula

The diagonal follows the Pythagorean theorem:

d = √(l² + w²)

Area = l × w · Perimeter = 2(l + w) · Circumcircle radius = d / 2.

Frequently asked questions

What is the rectangle diagonal formula?

d = √(width² + height²).

Does the diagonal bisect the angles?

Not generally. It bisects the rectangle's area, but only bisects corner angles when the rectangle is a square.
