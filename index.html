QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

How do I create a stylish nickname?

Type your name in the generator, browse the results and press the copy button on any card you like. You can adjust the intensity or search for a style too.

Will these names work in every app?

Most results are standard Unicode text and can be pasted into many apps. However, some services restrict certain characters or display them differently depending on device and font support.

Where are my saved favourites stored?

Favourites are stored in your browser using localStorage. They are not uploaded to a server. Clearing browser site data will remove them.

Is this website free to use?

Yes. The static generator runs in your browser and does not require an account.
